Strategy & Growth

Tyler Claro

Perspective 01 — Strategic Direction

Data-driven marketing strategist specializing in brand positioning, consumer insights, and digital growth strategy. Tyler earned his degree in Marketing Management from IPAM Porto and holds certifications in Content & Social Media Strategy from Adobe and Google AI Essentials.

Having led digital growth frameworks across 25+ real-world projects, his experience includes formulating the full B2B digital marketing plan for Insertpage's HR software (PointWatch), designing acquisition funnels for ClaroAuto and RCR Home Services, and conducting strategic market research for brands like Super Bock Group, Papillin, and Quinta do Monteverde.

Story & Craft

Jonathan Claro

Perspective 02 — Creative Direction

Creative director and audiovisual specialist focused on visual identity, cinematography, and high-impact media production. Jonathan earned his degree in Multimedia Communication Technology at Universidade da Maia (UMAIA), where he served as Head of Communication for AE UMAIA and Co-Founder & Vice-President of the Multimedia Student Nucleus (NEM).

Through his internship at Visuals by LUMA—executing camera operation, lighting direction, video editing, and audio capture—and producing the official Making-Of documentary and digital promotional content for the feature film "O Pecado Mora em Cima" (Station / RTP Studios), as well as creating the full visual identity and video campaigns for ClaroAuto, he transforms strategic ideas into compelling cinematic experiences across 25+ real-world projects.
